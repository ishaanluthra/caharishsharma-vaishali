How we handle it

GST Registration & Returns, the careful way

GST rewards discipline. A late GSTR-1 blocks your customers' input credit; claiming credit that is not in GSTR-2B invites a notice. We run a reconciliation every return period, so you claim every rupee of credit you are entitled to, and nothing you are not.

For new businesses we handle registration end to end, including the address-proof and verification steps that most often cause rejections. For existing ones we take over filings mid-year and clean up past mismatches.

FAQs

GST registration & returns: common questions

Have a question that is not here? Call 99719 92189 or send a WhatsApp.

When is GST registration mandatory?

Generally when aggregate turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services in a financial year (lower limits apply in some special category states). It is compulsory regardless of turnover for inter-state supply of goods, most e-commerce sellers, and a few other cases.

How long does GST registration take?

Once documents are complete, an application is usually approved within a few working days. If the officer raises a query or orders physical verification, it takes longer; we handle the replies.

What is the difference between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

GSTR-1 reports your outward sales invoice by invoice; your buyers' input credit depends on it. GSTR-3B is the summary return in which you pay the net tax after claiming input credit.

I have missed a few GST returns. What now?

File the pending returns as soon as possible with late fee and interest; long gaps can lead to suspension or cancellation of the GSTIN. Share your login and we will tell you exactly what is pending and what it will cost in late fee.
