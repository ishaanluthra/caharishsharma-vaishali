FAQs

Regime questions, answered

Is income up to ₹12.75 lakh tax-free for salaried people?

Under the new regime, the ₹75,000 standard deduction brings a ₹12.75 lakh salary down to ₹12 lakh taxable, where the rebate makes the tax nil. Income taxed at special rates, such as capital gains, is not covered by this.

When is the old regime better?

When your deductions and exemptions are large: HRA in a high-rent city, home loan interest, full 80C and 80D, NPS. The break-even moves with income, which is why it is worth running both numbers every year.

Can I switch regimes every year?

Salaried individuals without business income can choose the regime each year when filing the return. Those with business or professional income have limited options to switch, so the choice needs more care.

What is marginal relief?

If your taxable income is slightly above ₹12 lakh under the new regime, the tax payable is capped at the amount by which your income exceeds ₹12 lakh. The same idea applies at each surcharge threshold. The calculator applies both.
