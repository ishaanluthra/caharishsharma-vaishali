What happens if I miss a GST return date?

A late fee is charged per day of delay, and interest is charged on any tax paid late. A pending GSTR-1 also blocks the next one, and long gaps can lead to suspension of the GSTIN.

Who has to pay advance tax?

Anyone whose tax liability for the year, after TDS, is ₹10,000 or more. It is paid in instalments by 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March. Resident senior citizens without business income are exempt.

What is the ITR due date?

For most individuals whose accounts do not need an audit, the standard due date is 31 July after the end of the financial year. Audit cases have later dates. Always check for the current year's notification.
