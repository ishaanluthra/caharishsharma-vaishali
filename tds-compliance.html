FAQs

TDS returns & compliance: common questions

Have a question that is not here? Call 99719 92189 or send a WhatsApp.

When is TDS deposited and when are returns due?

TDS deducted in a month is generally deposited by the 7th of the next month (30 April for March). Quarterly returns are normally due by 31 July, 31 October, 31 January and 31 May. See our due-date calendar for upcoming dates.

What happens if I file a TDS return late?

A late fee applies for each day of delay, capped at the TDS amount, and a penalty can also be levied for longer delays. Interest is separately charged on late deduction or deposit.

I bought a flat. Do I need to deduct TDS?

Yes, if the property value is ₹50 lakh or more you generally deduct TDS from the payment to the seller and file the challan-cum-statement online. We can do this for you.

How do I fix a TRACES default notice?

We download the justification report, identify the cause (wrong PAN, short payment, late filing) and file a correction statement or pay the shortfall so the default is cleared.
