How we handle it

Accounting & Bookkeeping, the careful way

Good books are the difference between a fifteen-minute GST return and a weekend of searching for invoices. We record sales, purchases and expenses every month, reconcile your bank statements, and flag anything that looks wrong while it is still easy to fix.

At year end, your profit and loss account and balance sheet are ready for the income tax return, audit or loan file without a last-minute rush.

Monthly bookkeeping

Sales, purchases, expenses and journal entries posted every month.

Reconciliations

Bank, GST (books vs GSTR-1 / 2B) and party ledgers matched.

Reports

Monthly profit summary, outstanding receivables and payables.

Year-end finalisation

Profit and loss account, balance sheet and schedules for tax or audit.

FAQs

Accounting & bookkeeping: common questions

Have a question that is not here? Call 99719 92189 or send a WhatsApp.

Which accounting software do you work with?

We work in the software you already use, such as Tally, or can set up a simple system if you are starting fresh.

Do I need to send documents every month?

Yes. A monthly cycle keeps GST returns accurate and catches errors early. Most clients share invoices and statements on WhatsApp or email at the start of each month.

Can you clean up books from previous years?

Yes. We can rebuild past records from bank statements and invoices so your accounts match your filed returns before an audit, loan or notice reply.

Is my financial data kept confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality of client information is a professional obligation for chartered accountants, and your data is used only for your work.
