Company & LLP Registration, the careful way

Incorporation is the easy part. The first year brings an auditor appointment within 30 days, a commencement-of-business filing within 180 days, board meetings, director KYC and annual returns. Many new founders miss these and pay penalties later. We plan them from the start.

We also help you compare structures honestly: an LLP may suit a two-partner consultancy better than a company, while a private limited company is usually needed if you plan to raise investment.

FAQs

Company & LLP registration: common questions

Have a question that is not here? Call 99719 92189 or send a WhatsApp.

Private limited company or LLP: which is better?

A private limited company suits businesses that want to raise equity, issue shares to investors or employees, or grow fast. An LLP has lighter compliance and suits professional firms and small partnerships. We explain the trade-off for your specific plan.

How many people do I need to start a company?

A private limited company needs at least two directors and two shareholders (they can be the same people). An OPC needs one person plus a nominee. An LLP needs at least two designated partners.

What compliance is required after incorporation?

Key early steps are appointing the first auditor within 30 days, filing the commencement of business declaration (INC-20A) within 180 days, holding board meetings, and filing annual accounts and returns with the ROC every year.

Can I use my home address as the registered office?

Yes, a residential address can be the registered office with a recent utility bill and an NOC from the owner.
